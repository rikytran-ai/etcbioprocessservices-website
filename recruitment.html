Talent Services

Connecting bioprocess talent with biologics innovators.

We help biopharmaceutical and biotechnology companies find experienced bioprocess professionals, from individual contributors through senior technical leadership. Our network spans decades of industry relationships.

How we work.

We are not a generalist recruiting firm. We focus exclusively on bioprocess, cGMP, and quality compliance roles in biopharmaceutical, biotechnology, and biologics manufacturing. That depth lets us evaluate candidates against the real technical demands of your open positions.

Roles we typically support.

Process development scientists and engineers across upstream and downstream operations. Quality assurance and quality control professionals with bioprocess experience. Validation engineers across IQ, OQ, PQ, and continued process verification. Manufacturing science and technology leaders. Regulatory affairs professionals with biologics experience. Manufacturing leadership across mAbs, vaccines, plasma proteins, and emerging modalities.

What makes our placements last.

We assess technical fit, cultural fit, and career trajectory. We brief candidates honestly on the realities of the role, the team, and the technical environment. We brief our client companies on what each candidate genuinely brings, including the gaps that may need development support. Both sides start the engagement with clarity.
